FAQ

No screenshots show up. Check photo access under Settings › Apps › Checkame. Even without it, you can read screenshots from the share sheet ("Checkame").

A date was read wrong. Tap the date on the confirm card to fix it. The fix is reused next time, and each correction earns 1 point.

It says "please check". Is something wrong? Checkame doesn't judge. It only points out what doesn't line up. If the booking is what you intended, choose "This is intended".

I want to save to Google Calendar. Add your Google account under iPhone Settings › Apps › Calendar › Accounts, then pick Google Calendar as the destination in MY.

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. See the privacy policy.

I'm getting a new iPhone. With iCloud sync on, just sign in with the same Apple ID on the new iPhone. You can also export a file from MY › Backup / Restore and import it on the new iPhone (nothing is deleted; only missing items are added).

I don't want others to see the app. Choose Face ID / Touch ID or a PIN under Settings › App lock. The app locks when opened and when you return from another app.
