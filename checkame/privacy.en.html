Checkame Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026 (revised)

We never store your personal information. Your name, phone number, email and address never leave your iPhone and your own iCloud. Reading screenshots and cross-checking bookings normally happen on your iPhone. To improve recognition we keep only screenshots with personal information blacked out plus the reading results (you can turn this off in Settings).

1. Where things are stored

Screenshots, plans, numbers and memos (such as receipts) that Checkame reads are stored only on your iPhone and in your own iCloud (private database). iCloud sync can be turned off in Settings. We cannot see any of it.

2. Permissions

  • Photos: used only to read screenshots and ticket photos.
  • Calendar and Reminders: used to write the plans and deadlines you register and to check for duplicates.
  • Notifications: used to remind you the day before and on the day of a deadline.
  • Face ID / Touch ID (optional): used only to unlock the app when you turn on App lock in Settings. iOS handles the biometric data itself; the app and we never receive it.

You can use Checkame from the share sheet without granting any of these.

3. Improving recognition (what we store on our server)

To improve the reading rules, when the confirm card reads a screenshot, when you check it with AI, and when you register or skip, the app sends the following to our server (Google Cloud), where it is stored.

  • The screenshot that was read. Parts that are names, phone numbers, email addresses, addresses (postal codes) or card numbers are blacked out on your iPhone before sending.
  • The recognized text (with the same personal information masked), the reading result (dates, type, place, booking numbers), the values you corrected, actions such as register or skip, the app version and display language.
  • A random number generated by the app per installation. No account, device identifier or name is included, so we cannot tell whose data it is.

Not sent: names, contact details, addresses, other photos in your library, calendar contents, location. Booking numbers, dates, places and amounts remain as learning material. The data is used only to improve the reading rules and check quality, and is never sold or passed on to third parties. You can turn this off any time with "Help improve recognition" in Settings; when off, nothing is sent. To request deletion, contact support.

When you correct a date, small learning fragments (date wording, type, before/after values) are also received via Apple iCloud (public database) as before. The same setting turns them off.

3b. Cloud re-read (optional) and trip suggestions (optional)

Only when you tap "Check again with AI" on the confirm card, that single image is sent to our reading server (on Google Cloud) and read with Google's generative AI. The server stores neither the image nor the result; it only returns the result to your iPhone. Nothing is sent unless you tap.

(Not currently offered.) Only if you turn on "Trip suggestions" in Settings, your trip's destination, dates and party size are sent to the same server to return information near your hotel. Screenshots, booking numbers and names are never sent, and the server stores nothing.

4. Google integration (optional)

Only if you sign in to Google in MY and turn on "Save deadlines to Google Tasks", the title and date of a deadline you register are sent to your own Google account. Sign-in credentials are stored only on this iPhone and deleted when you sign out. Saving to a Google Calendar added to your iPhone goes through the iOS Calendar feature, with no direct communication with Google.

5. Sign in with Apple (optional)

This only displays your name on this iPhone. Signing in does not change where data is stored and nothing is sent to us.

5b. Kept only on this iPhone

  • Favorite places (home and work address): stored only on this iPhone and used only in the URL handed to the iOS Maps app when you open directions. Never sent to us.
  • App lock PIN: stored only in this iPhone's keychain and not synced to iCloud.

5c. File backup

The file you export from MY › Backup / Restore (events, trips, memos and corrections) leaves your iPhone only when you choose where to share it. We do not receive it. Screenshot images are not included.

6. What we don't collect

  • Accounts or passwords
  • Third-party SDKs such as analytics or crash reporting
  • Advertising or tracking
  • Location
